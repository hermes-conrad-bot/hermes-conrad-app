# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Hermes Conrad is a personal, single-user assistant. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

## Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app accesses:

- Google Calendar data (events, times, locations, descriptions) from the owner's account.
- Gmail messages (read-only) from the owner's account.

No other person's Google account is accessed.

## How data is used

Data is read only to carry out tasks the owner asks for (for example, checking the calendar before planning meals, or finding a relevant email). It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the owner's requests.

## Sharing

Data is not sold and is not shared with third parties, with one exception: to perform tasks, the content of calendar events and emails the app reads may be sent to a third-party AI model provider (currently Anthropic) for processing. That provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.

## Storage and security

The OAuth access token granting access to the Google account is stored on a private, self-hosted server controlled by the owner. Calendar and email content is read on demand and is not published or copied into any public location.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on this repository.
